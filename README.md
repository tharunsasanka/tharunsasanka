## `> active_projects`

### `01` — AI-Powered DFIR Investigation Assistant

> **Digital Forensics + Incident Response + AI**

A cybersecurity investigation platform designed to support structured forensic investigations and SOC-style workflows.

```text
LOG INGESTION
      ↓
ACTIVITY ANALYSIS
      ↓
TIMELINE RECONSTRUCTION
      ↓
SUSPICIOUS ACTIVITY
      ↓
MITRE ATT&CK MAPPING
      ↓
EVIDENCE MANAGEMENT
      ↓
AI-ASSISTED REPORTING
```

**Core Areas**

`Windows Logs` · `Linux Logs` · `Timeline Analysis` · `MITRE ATT&CK` · `Evidence` · `Threat Intelligence` · `Chain of Custody`

**Technology**

`React` `TypeScript` `Tailwind CSS` `FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `Docker`

---

### `02` — CyberPortfolio

> **Security-Focused Full-Stack Portfolio**

An animated cybersecurity portfolio platform with a public profile and protected management system.

**Features**

`Projects` · `Certificates` · `Skills` · `Cyber Labs` · `Contact Inbox` · `AI Assistant` · `Certificate Verification`

**Technology**

`React` `TypeScript` `Tailwind CSS` `MongoDB`

---

### `03` — Healthcare Cryptography System

> **Secure Application + Cryptography**

A healthcare application exploring authentication, authorization and protection of sensitive information.

**Security Areas**

`JWT` · `RBAC` · `AES Encryption` · `Medical Records` · `Security Analysis`

### `04` — IoT Flood Warning System

An ESP32-based monitoring solution combining multiple environmental sensors with automated alerting.

**Technology**

`ESP32` · `Water Level Sensor` · `Rain Sensor` · `Soil Moisture` · `Weather Data` · `Cloud Alerts`
